# Game overview (from the user's notes, 2026-11-06)

A plane game where you mass-murder goblins moving below, to protect humanity. The goal is to kill every last goblin on Earth.

## Loop
Goblins come out of their holes and move somewhere; the player has to kill them before they scurry underground. The player is
defending something, which could be the Pyramids, the Grand Canyon or a normal supermarket.

## Goblins
Most of the horde is passive and doesn't fight back. Armed goblins spawn in groups and fight back, shooting and firing RPGs.
Vehicles are a separate system that goblins may use, so they move faster or attack more (tanks and so on).

## Missions and run structure
Three mission types, each on an authored map:
1. Goblins attacking something, or trying to get into something.
2. Goblins raiding something (for example the supermarket): they go in and come out with something.
3. Protect a road where humans are travelling, to make sure cars get across safely.

The player doesn't get attached to the map; they get attached to their plane, so **upgrades belong to the plane** and the map
changes every time. A map lasts around 4 to 5 minutes, with an upgrade screen after every map.

## Upgrade screen: two versions, torn between them
1. **Pick Three (roguelike):** map order is random and each run makes a new build.
2. **Permanent upgrade tree (incremental / TD):** play each map until you beat it. Choose which weapons and abilities to take into
   the map, upgrade the weapons, and roll for abilities each map (abilities random, weapons chosen).

**Plan:** test the Pick Three version first. If it isn't fun, try the incremental one and play-test both. Whichever gets more
attention and feedback decides which one is made.
